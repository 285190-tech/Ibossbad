Unit 5.3 — The Industrial Revolution Begins (1750–1900)

Essential Question: Explain how environmental factors contributed to industrialization from 1750 to 1900.

  • Agricultural Improvements: Crop rotation, seed drill (Jethro Tull), and enclosure movement increased food supply and urban labor migration.
  • Natural Resources & Geography: Access to coal deposits, iron ore, navigable rivers, and colonial raw materials accelerated mechanized textile production in Britain.
  • Technological Innovations: James Watt's steam engine (1765), spinning jenny, and water frame shifted production from cottage industries to the factory system.
